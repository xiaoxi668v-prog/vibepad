# Bluetooth HID prototype acceptance

1. Pair once from macOS Bluetooth settings and reconnect without repeating pairing.
2. Move the pointer continuously for ten minutes without a stuck button or modifier.
3. Verify single click, right click, drag, vertical scroll, and horizontal scroll.
4. Verify Esc, Enter, Tab, Shift+Tab, Space, arrows, Y/N, Control+C, and Command+Enter.
5. Verify the Typeless control sends left Alt down on touch-down and releases it on touch-up.
6. Disconnect Bluetooth during a held modifier and confirm all keys are released after reconnect.
7. Record HID send failures, maximum input-report gap, and reconnect count.
8. Compare the subjective cursor continuity against Entangle over the same ten-minute session.
